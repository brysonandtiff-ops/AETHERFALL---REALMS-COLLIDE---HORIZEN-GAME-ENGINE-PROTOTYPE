## ThornDell — The Venomroot (CHR_ThornDell)

**Design state: APPROVED CONCEPT.** The owner expressly approved the detailed concept sheet before this evidence-intake request. Preserve the existing ID and the approval image.

**Visual signature:** enormous humanlike forest guardian with branching asymmetrical stag antlers, monumental shoulders, beard, living armoured bark, fractured basalt plates, aged bronze ornaments, bound roots, fungi, poison-sap vials and lime-green venom pathways. The antlered silhouette must remain recognisable against other tanks. Character carries a massive thorn-wrapped, root-grown **Root Bulwark**. Do not replace his poison/disruption role with an ordinary nature tank, and do not silently change his distinctive shield for an unrelated weapon.

**Gameplay direction (concept only):** venom tank/disruptor; illustrated actions are **Root Snare**, **Briar Armor** and **Toxic Overgrowth**. Descriptions or cooldown values on art are art pitches, not balanced playable abilities.

**Preview present:** a separate, low-poly, rotating blockout appears in an 850×900 MP4, with “offline VTK render” and “no rig / not Godot or Blender proof” printed on frames. Video pixels were inspected, *not* its claimed underlying GLB.

## Korvex — The Hollow Titan (CHR_Korvex)

**Design state:** provided concept reference; existing registry remains a legacy-origin AETHERFALL candidate pending a separately reconciled approval state. Do not automatically replace its known status with a rendered prototype.

**Visual signature:** immense gothic mausoleum-titan with heavy grave-iron/bone armour, hollow illuminated purple chest, monumental crown/helmet, glowing violet energy, chains, lanterns and a distinctive **Grave Maul**. Illustrative gameplay: attrition, anti-tank pressure and zone denial. The supplied Korvex blockout is a simplified, squat purple armoured shape with the maul. Preserve both records, and flag that it does not yet match the canonical-quality concept sheet.

**Preview present:** a front/three-quarter/back/weapon sheet and rotating GIF label themselves “actual exported GLB mesh”, rendered with **VTK 9.6**. Underlying GLB files and engine load tests were not supplied; exact mesh and the printed triangle count are not independently verified.

## Vellaris — The Bloodshield (CHR_Vellaris_Bloodshield)

**Design state: previously approved AETHERFALL concept** per existing repository registry. A regal white-haired vampiric-elven crimson tank in ornate metal armour and cape, red blood-magic barrier and a bloodshield crest; protective frontline/self-sustain identity, distinct from Korvex and ThornDell. The supplied concept board includes turnarounds, material callouts, weapon sketches and *proposed* abilities. It is not an implemented Godot character.

## Approval boundaries

Owner approval locks visual **direction**, not Blender topology, UVs, rigging, procedural model quality, Godot material fidelity, runtime playability, LODs, weapons, animation, balance or device acceptance. Preserve high-resolution concept masters and authorisation evidence through each future production stage.

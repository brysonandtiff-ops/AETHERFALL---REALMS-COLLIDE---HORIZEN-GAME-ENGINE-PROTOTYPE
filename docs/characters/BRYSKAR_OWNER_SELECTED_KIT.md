## Character combat identity

- **Role:** TANK
- **Subroles:** DIVE / RESCUE / FRONTLINE / PROTECTION
- **Archetype:** mobile frontline breach tank, intentionally protecting teammates even when doing so exposes him to danger.
- **Player fantasy:** lead the assault, break formations, intercept attacks on teammates, rescue allies, preserve team momentum.
- **Identity distinction:** mobility and active rescue—not a generic slow fortress tank or a copy of Vellaris's blood-magic shield.

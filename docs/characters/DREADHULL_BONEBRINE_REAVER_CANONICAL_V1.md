## Do not drift

- Broad asymmetrical heavy bipedal tank; hooded undead face with dark recess, narrow cyan glow and exposed cyan-lit skeletal ribs; no tentacle beard.
- Bone-brine plates with ivory curved whale-bone ribs, dark blue-black metal, rusted antique-gold ironwork, chains and bound salvage ropes.
- Tattered crimson-magenta sails and cloth drape over shoulder and waist; cyan brine lantern and magical energy glow.
- Ossuary Dredge: large arm-carried cylindrical salvage winch, circular ribbed bone-claw aperture and glowing cyan brine chamber; preserve exact weapon shape, not an anchor-scythe.
- Opposite arm retains the original strapped jagged timber-and-bone salvage bulwark shown in hero art and turnarounds.
- Original wreck frame mounted behind shoulders: open shipwreck timber rigging, rope webbing, mast fragments and hanging lantern; do NOT substitute a full galleon.

## Concept kit

- **Dredge Snare** (1): Fires chains and bone-ribs from the Ossuary Dredge to latch onto enemies and wreckage, dragging them toward Dreadhull and displacing their position.
- **Salvage Recall** (2): Pulls floating or shattered debris back into himself, restoring armor and briefly healing for a portion of the reclaimed wreckage.
- **Reforge Bulwark** (3): Converts dredged wreckage into a large bonebrine barricade, granting cover and a protective aura that reduces incoming damage for Dreadhull and nearby allies.
- **Wreckline Drag** (4): Whips a chain line from the dredge, dragging a targeted enemy along with nearby wreckage, briefly slowing them on arrival and disrupting their formation.
- **Graveyard Forge** (5_ultimate): Dredges a massive field of wreckage from the seafloor, erupting into a fortified salvage zone. The area becomes a graveyard forge that grants cover, empowers allies' defenses, and continuously reclaims debris over time.
- **Sunken Claim** (passive): Damage absorbed by his shields stores Salvage Charge. At high charge, his next defensive ability is strengthened (increased shield, duration, and additional effect).
